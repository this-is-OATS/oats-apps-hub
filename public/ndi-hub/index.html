OATS NDI HUB

NDI multiview and free PTZ control in any browser, a webcam as an NDI camera with PTZ and a protected mic, a display as NDI. For a Mac on your NDI network.

⬇ Download for MacREAD ME FIRSTv0.1.0 · 2026-10-25

What it does

  • OATS Hub: every NDI source on the network in a browser multiview (phone, iPad, laptop), click to select with tally, joystick / zoom / 8 presets / autofocus for any NDI PTZ camera. No license, no paid add-on. Wall-monitor grid at /multiview.
  • Camera sender: an Insta360 Link 2 (or any UVC webcam) as an NDI source WITH PTZ, no vendor app needed. Mic in the same feed, muted at start, with howl detection, a speech-only voice mode and a monitoring-loop detector.
  • Display sender: one of the Mac's displays as a steady 30 fps NDI source.
  • Installs to ~/Applications/OATS NDI HUB with a login item; re-run the installer to update, settings and presets are kept.

You need

  • Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later.
  • NDI Tools (free) from ndi.video/tools for the NDI runtime.
  • Rosetta 2 for the camera and display senders (one Terminal command, in the read-me).

Install

unzip, then in Terminal:
"/path/to/OATS NDI HUB/install.sh"

then open  http://<the Mac>:7080/  on anything on the same Wi-Fi.
iPad: Safari › Share › Add to Home Screen.
