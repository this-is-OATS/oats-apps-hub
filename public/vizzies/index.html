VJ VIZZIES

A headless clip + fractal player for shows: driven from grandMA3 (sACN / OSC), full screen over HDMI to LED walls and projectors, and over NDI for previs.

⬇ Download for MacREAD ME FIRSTv1.0 · 2026-10-23

What it does

  • Fractal + clip player at 60 fps on an Apple Silicon Mac mini, output window on the HDMI show display.
  • sACN universe + OSC control from grandMA3; browser controls at http://<the Mac>:8090 from the lighting network.
  • NDI previs 'OATS VIZ Program' (Vision and other receivers); NDI problems never stop the HDMI output.
  • Mac app with menu-bar health dot, start / stop / restart, logs, open at login.

You need

  • Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later.
  • Optional: Rosetta 2 (NDI helper mode) and NDI Tools (NDI previs). Shows over HDMI need neither.

Install

Unzip "VJ VIZZIES.zip", drag VJ VIZZIES.app to Applications.
First time: right-click › Open › Open (ad-hoc signed).
If macOS says it is damaged:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/VJ VIZZIES.app"
