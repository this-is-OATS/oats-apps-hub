VJ VIZZIES SUITE

The show laptop package: the VJ VIZZIES clip + fractal player (grandMA3 sACN / OSC, full screen over HDMI, NDI previs) plus the OATS NDI HUB camera suite (multiview, free PTZ, webcams as NDI cameras, tally). One app: drag it to Applications, open it, answer two questions.

Install

  1. Download the .dmg and open it.
  2. Drag VJ VIZZIES to the Applications folder next to it.
  3. Open VJ VIZZIES from Applications. First time on a Mac, macOS says it cannot verify the app (it is not notarized yet): open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, then open the app again. Once per Mac; updates from inside the app need no second time.
  4. Answer two questions: this Mac's NDI machine name (must differ from every other Mac on the network) and the lighting network interface. Click Install.
  5. Click Allow when macOS asks for the camera, the microphone and the local network (once; the app asks itself, the cameras it starts inherit it). If the NDI runtime is missing, it asks for your password once to install it.
  6. Done. The hub is at http://<the Mac>:7080/, the player controls at http://<the Mac>:8090, the app opens at login and starts everything.

What it does

  • VJ VIZZIES: fractal + clip player at the display rate, output window on the HDMI show display; sACN universe + OSC from grandMA3; browser controls at http://<the Mac>:8090 from the lighting network; NDI previs 'OATS VIZ Program'; the Mac app is the controls window with a menu-bar health dot (start / stop / restart, logs, open at login); a Windows launcher for the PC is in the zip.
  • OATS NDI HUB: every NDI source in a browser multiview with click-to-select tally, joystick / zoom / presets / autofocus / AI tracking for any NDI PTZ camera; an Insta360 Link 2, an OBSBOT or any UVC webcam as an NDI camera WITH PTZ and a protected mic (one sender per camera); a display as NDI; tally on a phone page, the OBSBOT LED or a lighting console; OSC input on 7081.
  • Installs to ~/Applications (VJ VIZZIES, OATS NDI HUB). Open the app again after an update: it updates in place, your settings, presets, scenes, camera profiles and media are kept. Check for Updates… in the app downloads the next version from here and replaces itself.

You need

  • A Mac with macOS 14 or later: Apple Silicon or Intel (the 2020 MacBook Pro is a supported target); universal binaries, nothing to compile. The fractal governor lowers the internal resolution on a weaker GPU to hold the frame rate.
  • The NDI runtime: NDI Tools (free, ndi.video/tools) installs it, and the app carries a copy for Macs where Tools left it out.
  • Apple Silicon only: Rosetta 2 (the app installs it with the runtime when it is missing).

Scripted install (the zip)

unzip, then   ./INSTALL.command --yes --machine-name LAPTOP --lighting-nic en0      (preview: --dry-run)
or double-click INSTALL.command and answer the two questions in Terminal.
The same scripts live inside the app:  "/Applications/VJ VIZZIES.app/Contents/Resources/suite/INSTALL.command"
