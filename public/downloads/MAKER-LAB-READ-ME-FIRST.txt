MAKER-LAB: READ ME FIRST
========================

MAKER-LAB is the embroidery bench for a Brother PE-770: it builds stitch files (satin lettering in the Mac's fonts, fills, borders, pictures),
shows them as design / stitches / real thread before anything is sewn, checks them against the machine's rules, and copies the one you pick onto
the USB stick. It is one Mac app with everything inside: nothing else to install.

What you need
-------------
1. An Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) with macOS 13 (Ventura) or later.
2. Optional: the "claude" command line tool, for the chat bar at the bottom of the pages. Everything else works without it.
3. A USB stick named EMBROIDER (FAT32) if you want it to load files for a Brother PE-770.

Install
-------
1. Open "MAKER-LAB-<version>.dmg" and drag MAKER-LAB onto the Applications folder next to it.
2. The first time, macOS refuses it ("Apple could not verify MAKER-LAB is free of malware"): it is signed with its own certificate, not by an
   Apple developer account. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the line about MAKER-LAB and click "Open Anyway", then Open.
   (On macOS 14 and earlier, right-click MAKER-LAB in Applications > Open > Open does the same.) If macOS says the app is damaged, run in Terminal:
       xattr -d com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/MAKER-LAB.app"
3. If macOS asks whether MAKER-LAB may access removable volumes (the stick), click Allow. If it ever asks for "App Management", turn
   MAKER-LAB on under System Settings > Privacy & Security > App Management.

Updates
-------
The app looks for a newer version a few seconds after it opens and every 45 minutes. It downloads the update in the background, checks it, and
installs it once the Mac has had no keyboard or mouse for ten minutes and the bench has nothing running (no build, no copy to the stick), then
restarts itself. MAKER-LAB menu > "Check for Updates..." does it now; "Install Updates Automatically" turns the waiting off. The header of every
page shows the version and build, for example "Embroidery · Brother PE-770 · 1.1.0 (01a6036)".

Where things live
-----------------
- The app: /Applications/MAKER-LAB.app (the app replaces itself there; do not run it from the disk image).
- Everything it makes or keeps: ~/Library/Application Support/MAKER-LAB (reports, uploads, the glyph cache, logs). An update never touches it.
- Stitch files it builds: the MAKER-LAB folder inside the design folder it finds (Google Drive > HOBBYLAND > EMBROIDER on the author's Mac).

Not included
------------
Mac fonts are read from the Mac itself; the app ships none. The first Build in a font takes longer while the app measures its letters once.
